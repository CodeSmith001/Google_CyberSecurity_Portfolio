# Portfolio Activity 2: Internal Security Audit and Compliance Checklist

## 📝 Objective
The objective of this project was to conduct a simulated internal IT security audit for a growing e-commerce business. The goal was to assess the company's current security posture, identify vulnerabilities, and evaluate compliance with critical industry regulations using the **National Institute of Standards and Technology Cybersecurity Framework (NIST CSF)**.

## 🛠️ Skills Demonstrated
- Security Auditing & GRC (Governance, Risk, and Compliance)
- Risk Assessment & Threat Mitigation
- Regulatory Compliance Analysis (PCI DSS, GDPR)
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework Application
- Evaluating Security Controls (Administrative, Technical, and Physical)

## 🏢 Scenario: Botium Toys Internal Audit
**Situation:** 
Botium Toys, a small U.S.-based toy company with a physical storefront, is experiencing rapid growth in their global online market. The IT department is facing increased pressure to support this expansion. The IT Manager recognized the need to secure the company's expanding infrastructure and ensure compliance with regulations regarding online payments and European Union (E.U.) customer data.

**Task:** 
My task was to review the IT manager's established scope, goals, and initial risk assessment report. Following this review, I needed to perform an internal audit by completing a comprehensive controls and compliance checklist to identify current security gaps and potential regulatory liabilities.

## 🚀 Action Taken
To execute this internal audit, I took the following steps:
1. **Scope & Asset Review:** Analyzed the provided IT assets, audit scope, and business goals to understand Botium Toys' critical infrastructure and operational needs.
2. **Risk Assessment Analysis:** Reviewed the existing risk assessment to identify primary threats, vulnerabilities, and potential impacts to business continuity.
3. **Controls Evaluation:** Assessed the company's current security implementations against standard administrative, technical, and physical controls (e.g., least privilege, encryption, physical access locks).
4. **Compliance Checking:** Evaluated the company's practices against major regulatory requirements, specifically focusing on **PCI DSS** (for processing internal and online payments) and **GDPR** (for conducting business with E.U. citizens). 
5. **Documentation:** Completed the internal audit checklist, clearly documenting compliant areas and flagging non-compliant areas that pose a risk to the business.

## 📈 Results
The completed audit checklist provided a clear, documented overview of Botium Toys' security posture. By identifying specific gaps in controls and highlighting areas of non-compliance (such as potential PCI DSS and GDPR violations), this audit gives the IT manager actionable insights needed to implement necessary security upgrades, mitigate risks, and avoid potential regulatory fines.
